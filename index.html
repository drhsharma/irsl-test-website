Mechanics • Metamaterials • Reconfigurable Structures • Robotics

Engineering structures that adapt, transform, and respond through mechanics.

IRSL develops mechanically intelligent structures, origami-inspired systems, nonlinear vibration isolation concepts, architected metamaterials, and robotic mechanisms for adaptive engineering applications.

Research Themes

What we build and study

01

Mechanical Intelligence

Structures that use geometry, instability, energy landscapes, and passive transitions to respond without external control.

02

Reconfigurable Structures

Origami-inspired, deployable, transformable, and morphable structures for compact storage and functional shape change.

03

Architected Metamaterials

Mechanically programmed cellular materials with unusual deformation, stiffness, and extensibility responses.

04

Dynamics & Vibration Control

Nonlinear vibration isolation, quasi-zero-stiffness mechanisms, passive resonance mitigation, and adaptive protection systems.
